# arcane-3d-model

This definitely has to be one of my most favorite projects yet and it turned out amazing. Let me take you through how I created this. I started off looking for what I wanted to make and thought why not make something arcane related. I was thinking what should I make though so I browsed through pictures of arcane objects and came across a hexcore. Heres how it looks like: 

<img width="469" height="469" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-07 181857" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7261d307-95aa-4d66-b8de-13a1fda512ab" />

I first started of going to onshape and trying to create a pyramid. This was strangely one of my most hardest parts because I genuinely couldn't figure out how to create the pyramid until i discovered chamfer. Basically you can create a cube and chamfer will make the edges of it diagonal, which perfectly made my pyramid. Here: 

<img width="459" height="368" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-06 191405" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09aa583c-1cfa-4c6a-9aa2-2b76c816b74d" />

<img width="393" height="299" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-06 191419" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/67451d74-1370-47a3-bd6c-d6a220871e05" />

Then I worked on adding the gold designs as seen in the picture below:
This was my reference pic for the gold designs btw. The gold design took very very long because I had to measure every single one so that all of the corner pieces looked exactly the same. I drew my sketches on the faces of the pyramid and extruded it by 0.02 in to form the corner pieces. And for the lines on the edges of the pyramid I just drew 2 lines next to that edge and then also extruded it by 0.02 in. For the corner pieces I had to re-do it and for some reason mirror couldn't work because everytime i tried it it would delete my sketch henceforth why I had to draw each corner piece painstakingly by hand.

# ASSEMBLY TIME!!!
This took really long ~5-6 hours I think maybe even more I dont truly remember
I was experimenting around to see how I would be printing this out and at first i thought I should just print out 18 pyramids and 8 triangles but then I was like why don't I just assemble all of them together and print it out at once? So I was trying to find a way to copy and paste my parts and then discover the assembly tab in onshape which im so ever grateful for. I started off building a base (which I had to redo 3 times) By making a circle and a circle inside it on the top and right planes then extruding it.
